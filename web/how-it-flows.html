How the tool works — a flowchart

Follow the arrows: your inputs go in the top, the tool loops through the middle one part at a time, and your answer comes out the bottom.

input / output a step a decision ↻ loop back

The same thing in one line each

Inputs. Your rough low/high guess for the average height, a spread guess, how many parts you'll test, and your rig's lowest height — plus a break/survive for each drop as you go.
Check. Refuses a nonsense setup before any part is wasted.
The loop (once per part). Pick the most useful next height → drop the part → record what happened → if parts remain, feed that back and repeat. It works in three stages as it learns: find the zone, close the gap, then refine (testing slightly off-centre — the one clever trick — so it learns the average and the spread at once).
Fit. When the parts run out, fit the best bell curve and work out how sure to be.
Outputs. Average and spread with confidence ranges, the all-fire / no-fire heights, a chart, and a calculator for reliability, height, or "how many parts do I need?".
