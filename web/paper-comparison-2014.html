Neyer vs. 3POD, head to head

Ray, Roediger & Neyer

Ray, Roediger & Neyer (2014). "Comparison of the Neyer D-Optimal and 3pod Sensitivity Test Designs." JSM Proceedings, Section on Statistics in Defense and National Security.

Why it matters

A careful side-by-side of the 1994 method and its 2014 challenger — notable because Neyer himself is a co-author. So it's the original camp testing the newcomer's claims rather than taking them on faith, which makes the comparison unusually credible.

Think of it as: the reigning champion agreeing to a fair, refereed match against the challenger and publishing the scorecard.
How it connects to your drop test: if your instructor asks "why Neyer and not the newer 3POD?", this is the paper to cite — an even-handed comparison showing where each wins. For measuring the whole distribution (average + spread), Neyer holds up well; for one extreme tail, 3POD pulls ahead.
