Paul Roediger

Roediger, P. (2020). "Gonogo: An R Implementation of Test Methods to Perform, Analyze and Simulate Sensitivity Experiments." arXiv:2011.11177.

Why it matters to you

A free, open, documented program that runs the whole family — Neyer's method and the newer 3POD2.0 — in one place. You can feed it the same example you're targeting and use its answer as an independent second opinion on your own build.

Think of it as: a worked-solutions key. You build your own, then check it against a trusted free version that already does the same job.
How it connects to your drop test: a practical cross-check. If you ever doubt a number your tool gives, you can run the same drop heights and outcomes through gonogo and see if it agrees. Agreement between two independent programs is strong evidence for your instructor.
